NIMBUS ANALYTICS — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 3.0 | January 25, 2024 | INTERNAL — ALL EMPLOYEES

OWNER: Security Team (CISO office) | Approved by: Priya Raghavan, CTO

SCOPE

Applies to all employees, contractors, and systems that touch Nimbus or customer
data. Annual security training is mandatory; completion is tracked by People Ops.

ACCESS CONTROL

  — Single sign-on with phishing-resistant hardware keys (YubiKey) is required for
    all internal systems. Passwords live only in the company 1Password vault.
  — Production access is brokered exclusively through Gatekeeper. Direct SSH to
    production hosts is prohibited; all sessions are recorded.
  — Access follows least privilege and is re-certified in quarterly access reviews.
    Offboarding must revoke all access within 24 hours of departure.

DATA CLASSIFICATION

  PUBLIC      Marketing material, published docs.
  INTERNAL    Default for company information (this policy, runbooks, wikis).
  RESTRICTED  Customer event data, compensation data, board and legal material.

Customer event data is retained for 13 months and then deleted from Skyhook and
helios-db backups on a rolling schedule. Restricted data may not be exported to
personal devices or unapproved SaaS tools.

ENDPOINTS AND VENDORS

  — Laptops run the managed image: full-disk encryption, screen lock, MDM agent.
  — New vendors require a security review and a signed DPA before any data flows.

INCIDENT REPORTING

Suspected incidents must be reported in #incidents or to security@nimbus within
24 hours of discovery. The on-call incident commander (see On-Call Runbook) owns
triage; Security owns forensics and disclosure decisions.
