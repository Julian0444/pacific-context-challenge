May 28, 2024 | RESTRICTED — EXECUTIVE TEAM ONLY

PREPARED BY: Yuki Tanaka, VP Product | Ratified at the May 21 exec offsite

REVISION NOTES

Final roadmap; supersedes Draft v0.4 (May 6, 2024). Decisions taken at the
offsite: EU data residency PULLED FORWARD from Q4 to Q3 (three enterprise deals,
combined $640K ACV, are contractually contingent on it); warehouse-native mode
moves from Q3 to Q4; the AI assistant ships as a Q3 BETA renamed "Nimbus IQ"
(legal flagged the "Copilot" name); mobile session replay is CUT from FY2025.

FINAL SEQUENCE

  Q2 FY2025   Foundations: Frankfurt region procurement, data-partitioning
              design for residency, Nimbus IQ model-cost spike, enterprise
              tier hardening (SSO/SCIM gaps from Q1 deals).

  Q3 FY2025   EU DATA RESIDENCY GA — Frankfurt for Skyhook and helios-db,
              residency-aware routing in Gatekeeper.
              NIMBUS IQ BETA — AI insights assistant behind a flag for design
              partners; pricing decision deferred until beta telemetry.

  Q4 FY2025   WAREHOUSE-NATIVE MODE GA — Snowflake first, BigQuery fast-follow.
              Nimbus IQ GA decision gate.

  CUT FROM FY2025: Mobile session replay (revisit in FY2026 planning).

DEPENDENCIES AND RISKS

  — Residency work assumes both SRE hires (Hiring Plan FY2025) start by Q3;
    a slip pushes GA into early Q4 and endangers the $640K contingent ACV.
  — Nimbus IQ unit economics: beta gate requires model cost under 4% of the
    attributable ACV before GA pricing is set.
  — Warehouse-native performance target: p95 dashboard query under 3 seconds
    on partner datasets, or GA slips.

COMMUNICATION

Sales may commit to Q3 EU residency with named accounts under NDA. Nimbus IQ
may be teased as "AI insights beta, H2" without naming dates in contracts.
