NIMBUS ANALYTICS — PERFORMANCE REVIEW GUIDE FY2024
People Operations | February 28, 2024 | INTERNAL — ALL EMPLOYEES

OWNER: Ingrid Solberg, VP People

CYCLE OVERVIEW

Nimbus runs two review cycles per year, closing in April and October. Each cycle
has four steps: self-review, peer feedback, manager narrative, and calibration.
Promotions are decided only inside these windows; off-cycle promotions require
exec approval.

RATINGS

Performance uses a 5-level scale: Redefines, Exceeds, Meets, Approaching, Below.
Calibration guidance (not a forced curve): roughly 10% Redefines, 25% Exceeds,
50% Meets, 10% Approaching, 5% Below across a calibrated population. Ratings are
calibrated in panels chaired by the department head with People Ops in the room.

PROCESS AND TIMELINE (APRIL CYCLE)

  Week 1    Self-review submitted (impact against your 30-60-90 or OKRs).
  Weeks 1-2 Peer feedback: each employee nominates 3–5 peers; managers may add.
  Week 3    Manager narrative drafted: evidence-based, examples over adjectives.
  Week 4    Calibration panels; ratings finalized; promotion cases decided.
  Week 5    Delivery conversations. Written summaries land in Workday.

COMPENSATION LINKAGE

Merit increases and equity refreshes are budgeted after calibration closes.
Compensation adjustments follow the published compensation bands (band data is
manager-level access; employees see their own outcome letter). Promotion raises
move the employee to at least the 25th percentile of the target band.

EXPECTATIONS FOR MANAGERS

  — No surprises: concerns must have appeared in 1:1s before the cycle.
  — Narratives cite shipped work, incident contributions, and collaboration.
  — Peer feedback is confidential; quoting it verbatim is prohibited.
