QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS

  — Enterprise tier launched in February: 58 customers migrated or signed,
    already 34% of new bookings. Two lighthouse logos signed multi-year.
  — Reliability program delivered: automated helios-db failover shipped after
    the March 4 outage; RTO cut from 45 minutes to 8. SLA credits of $214K
    were absorbed in Q1 COGS; no enterprise churn attributable to the incident.
  — SOC 2 Type II renewed with zero major exceptions — now a sales asset in
    security reviews.

RISKS DISCUSSED WITH THE BOARD

  1. Pricing pressure: two competitors moved to aggressive usage-based pricing;
     win rates dipped in deals under $30K ACV. Packaging review underway.
  2. Concentration in product analytics: the roadmap bets on EU data residency
     and warehouse-native mode to open enterprise and European pipelines
     (see Product Roadmap FY2025 — Final).
  3. Hiring pace: 24 net-new heads planned (Hiring Plan FY2025); enterprise AE
     hiring is gated on Q2 net-new ARR.

ASKS AND RESOLUTIONS

The board approved the FY2025 operating plan and the equity refresh pool, and
reviewed the workforce contingency memo (Project Alpine) WITHOUT approving any
action — it remains a contingency gated on Q2 results.
