NIMBUS ANALYTICS — HIRING PLAN FY2025
People Operations & Finance | May 14, 2024 | RESTRICTED — MANAGERS AND ABOVE

OWNERS: Ingrid Solberg (VP People), Marcus Bell (CFO)

HEADCOUNT SUMMARY

FY2025 authorizes 24 net-new hires against a current base of ~180 employees,
weighted to engineering and revenue roles. Marketing backfills are FROZEN for
FY2025; any exception goes to the CFO.

  Engineering        11   (4 platform, 3 product eng, 2 SRE, 2 data)
  Sales               6   (4 AEs enterprise tier, 2 SDRs)
  Customer Success    4   (2 CSMs, 1 onboarding, 1 support eng)
  G&A                 3   (1 finance, 1 people ops, 1 IT)

The 2 SRE hires are tied to the reliability investment that followed the March
helios-db outage (postmortem PM-2024-03): 24/7 coverage without heroics and
ownership of the failover orchestrator.

TIMING AND GATES

  — Target start dates: 70% of offers accepted by end of Q3 FY2025.
  — Enterprise-tier AE hiring is gated on Q2 net-new ARR performance; if the
    Q2 gate is missed, the last 2 AE requisitions convert to Q4 decisions.
  — Every requisition needs a written role charter before it opens.

BUDGET

  — Recruiting budget: $380,000 for the year (tools, sourcing, events).
  — Agency usage is capped at 20% of hires; agency fees negotiated to 18%.
  — Offer approvals: hiring manager → department VP → People Ops (band check,
    see Compensation Bands 2024) → CFO for any out-of-band component.

REPORTING

People Ops publishes a monthly hiring dashboard (Prism board "hiring-fy2025")
tracking open reqs, pipeline pass-through, offer-accept rate, and time-to-fill.
