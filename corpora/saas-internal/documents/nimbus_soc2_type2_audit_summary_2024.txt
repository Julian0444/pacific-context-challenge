NIMBUS ANALYTICS — SOC 2 TYPE II AUDIT SUMMARY
Security & Compliance | April 30, 2024 | RESTRICTED — MANAGERS AND ABOVE

OWNER: CISO office | Auditor: Hartley & Voss LLP

RESULT

Nimbus received a SOC 2 Type II report with ZERO major exceptions for the audit
window June 1, 2023 – March 31, 2024, covering the Security, Availability, and
Confidentiality trust criteria. Two minor findings were noted (below). The full
report is available to customers under NDA through the trust portal.

SCOPE

Systems in scope: Skyhook (ingestion), Prism (dashboards), Gatekeeper (access
broker), helios-db (primary datastore), corporate identity and endpoint
management. The March 4 helios-db outage was reviewed under Availability; the
auditor accepted the postmortem and remediation evidence (automated failover,
Runbook v2) as an effective corrective-action process.

MINOR FINDINGS

  F-1  Offboarding latency. 4 of 31 sampled departures had access-revocation
       tickets closed more than 5 business days after the departure date,
       against a 24-hour policy (Information Security Policy v3). Remediation:
       automated offboarding workflow triggered from the HRIS, due Q3 FY2025.
       Owner: IT + People Ops.

  F-2  Log retention gap. Prism service logs were retained 90 days in one
       region versus the documented 180-day standard. Remediation: retention
       policy unified in the logging pipeline, due Q3 FY2025. Owner: SRE.

CUSTOMER-FACING GUIDANCE

Sales and CS may state: "SOC 2 Type II, zero major exceptions, report available
under NDA." Do not paraphrase the minor findings to customers; direct security
questionnaires to the compliance queue.

NEXT CYCLE

The FY2026 audit window opens July 1, 2024 (rolling 9-month window). Evidence
collection is continuous via the compliance automation platform.
