NIMBUS ANALYTICS — INCIDENT POSTMORTEM PM-2024-03
helios-db Primary Outage — March 4, 2024
Published March 9, 2024 | INTERNAL — ALL EMPLOYEES | BLAMELESS

INCIDENT COMMANDER: Lena Fischer | SCRIBE: on-call secondary

SUMMARY

On March 4, 2024, the helios-db primary Postgres cluster was unavailable or
severely degraded for 3 hours 42 minutes (09:14–12:56 UTC). Dashboards (Prism)
served stale data and event ingestion (Skyhook) buffered to disk. Approximately
38% of customers experienced dashboard latency or query failures. No events were
lost; buffered ingestion replayed fully by 14:30 UTC.

CUSTOMER AND BUSINESS IMPACT

  — 246 of ~640 customers affected (38%).
  — SLA credits issued: $214,000 against Q1 revenue.
  — 3 enterprise prospects in active evaluation were affected; CS notified.

ROOT CAUSE

A schema migration shipped at 09:02 UTC took an ACCESS EXCLUSIVE lock on the
events table. Long-running analytical queries queued behind the lock, exhausting
the pgbouncer connection pool within 12 minutes. Health checks then failed and
the API tier began timing out. Failover was NOT attempted for 68 minutes because
the documented procedure was manual (Runbook v1, RTO 45 minutes) and the on-call
engineer initially diagnosed the issue as an application bug.

WHAT WENT WELL / WHAT DIDN'T

  + Skyhook disk buffering prevented data loss.
  — Manual failover and a 15-minute paging acknowledgement window delayed response.
  — No incident-commander role: three engineers debugged, nobody owned comms.

ACTION ITEMS

  1. Automate helios-db failover (owner: SRE) — DONE, shipped April 2024;
     codified in On-Call Runbook v2 (RTO now 8 minutes).
  2. Migration review gate: any migration touching events requires SRE sign-off
     and lock-timeout settings — DONE.
  3. pgbouncer pool sizing and per-role limits — DONE.
  4. Adopt severity matrix and IC role — DONE (Runbook v2).
