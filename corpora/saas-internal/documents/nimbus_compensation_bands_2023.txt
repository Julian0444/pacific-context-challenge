PURPOSE

Base salary bands for software engineering, effective April 1, 2023. Bands are
confidential manager-level material: share outcomes with your reports, never the
band tables themselves.

BASE SALARY BANDS — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING (USD, ANNUAL)

  L3  Software Engineer          $118,000 – $142,000   (mid $130,000)
  L4  Senior Software Engineer   $145,000 – $172,000   (mid $158,500)
  L5  Staff Software Engineer    $178,000 – $205,000   (mid $191,500)
  EM1 Engineering Manager        $165,000 – $195,000   (mid $180,000)

Adjacent functions (SRE, Data) map to the same bands with a +5% premium at L4
and above, reflecting 2023 market scarcity.

BONUS AND EQUITY (2023 POLICY)

  — Company bonus target: 8% of base, paid annually on company performance.
  — Equity refreshes are AD HOC: managers nominate, execs approve case by case.
    There is no scheduled refresh program in 2023.

GEOGRAPHY

Single national band: no geographic tiers in 2023. Remote hires in any US state
are paid from the same table. (International contractors are out of scope.)

PLACEMENT GUIDANCE

  — New hires target the 25th–50th percentile of the band.
  — Placement above the 75th percentile requires VP People sign-off.
  — Out-of-band offers require CFO approval and a written exception memo.
