NIMBUS ANALYTICS — PROJECT ALPINE: WORKFORCE CONTINGENCY MEMO
June 3, 2024 | RESTRICTED — EXECUTIVE TEAM ONLY | DO NOT FORWARD

PREPARED BY: Dana Whitfield (CEO), Ingrid Solberg (VP People), Marcus Bell (CFO)

STATUS

CONTINGENCY PLANNING ONLY. No reduction has been approved. This memo defines the
trigger, scope, and execution standards so that a decision, if ever taken, is
fast and humane. Distribution is limited to the executive team.

TRIGGER

If Q2 FY2025 net-new ARR misses plan by more than 25% (plan: $1.9M net-new),
the executive team convenes a decision gate on JULY 15, 2024. Absent that miss,
Project Alpine is shelved at the July gate.

SCOPE (IF TRIGGERED)

  — Reduction of approximately 9% of workforce (~16 of ~180 roles).
  — Concentrated in marketing programs and G&A; engineering, SRE, and the
    enterprise sales pod are explicitly protected. The FY2025 hiring plan
    would be re-baselined (marketing freeze already in effect).
  — No performance framing: this would be a structural, role-based reduction.

SEVERANCE AND SUPPORT STANDARDS

  — 8 weeks base severance plus 2 weeks per full year of tenure.
  — Healthcare coverage continuation for 4 months (COBRA paid).
  — Accelerated vesting of the next quarterly tranche; 12-month exercise window.
  — Job-placement support contracted in advance; references from managers.

EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

Single-day notification, individually delivered by the direct manager with
People Ops present; same-day all-hands with written FAQ; no phased or rolling
cuts. Access revocation follows the offboarding policy without punitive haste.

COMMUNICATION RISK

Premature leakage would damage trust and Q2 sales. Nothing in this memo may be
referenced outside the executive team until the July 15 gate.
