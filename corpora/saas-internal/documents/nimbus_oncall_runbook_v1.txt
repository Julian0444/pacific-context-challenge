OWNER: Lena Fischer, Head of SRE

ROTATION

One primary and one secondary engineer per week, per service group (Skyhook,
Prism, helios-db). Handoff happens Mondays at 10:00 in #incidents. PagerBot,
our legacy Slack paging bot, delivers alerts to the primary.

ESCALATION (v1)

  1. Primary acknowledges the PagerBot alert. If no acknowledgement within
     15 minutes, PagerBot pages the secondary.
  2. If the incident is customer-visible and unresolved after 30 minutes,
     page Lena Fischer (Head of SRE) directly by phone.
  3. Executive notification is manual: the on-call engineer emails the CTO
     for anything affecting more than 10% of customers.

DATABASE FAILOVER (helios-db)

Failover to the standby replica is a MANUAL procedure documented in the
db-failover wiki page. Expected recovery time objective (RTO): 45 minutes,
including manual DNS switch, replica promotion, and connection-pool restart.
Only SRE members with Gatekeeper production grants may execute it.

SEVERITY

Incidents are informally classified as "minor" or "major" at the discretion
of the on-call engineer. Major incidents require a written summary in
#incidents within 48 hours.

KNOWN GAPS (ACKNOWLEDGED AT PUBLICATION)

  — No automated failover for helios-db.
  — No formal severity matrix or incident-commander role.
  — PagerBot has no override for silenced channels.
