NIMBUS ANALYTICS — ENGINEERING ONBOARDING GUIDE
People Operations & Engineering Enablement
Effective February 12, 2024 | INTERNAL — ALL EMPLOYEES

OWNER: Tomás Aguirre, VP Engineering

WELCOME

Every engineering hire completes a 4-day onboarding bootcamp during their first week,
run twice a month by Engineering Enablement. By day 5 you should have shipped a small
change to production through the full pipeline.

FIRST-WEEK CHECKLIST

  Day 1: Laptop setup (managed image, disk encryption enforced), SSO enrollment with
         hardware security key, 1Password vault, Slack channels (#eng-announce,
         #helios-db, #incidents, #ship-it).
  Day 2: Clone the nimbus-core monorepo, run the local stack, pair with your
         onboarding buddy (assigned before your start date).
  Day 3: Bootcamp sessions — architecture tour: Skyhook (event ingestion pipeline),
         Prism (dashboard service), Gatekeeper (auth and production access broker),
         helios-db (primary Postgres cluster).
  Day 4: Ship your starter task through Skyship, our continuous-delivery system.
  Day 5: Retro with your manager; 30-60-90 plan agreed and written down.

HOW WE SHIP

  — Trunk-based development: short-lived branches, merged behind feature flags.
  — Skyship deploys on every green merge; production deploys freeze on Fridays.
  — Every service has a runbook; on-call is opt-in before your 90th day.

30-60-90 EXPECTATIONS

  30 days: independent contributions in one service area, first on-call shadow.
  60 days: own a small project end to end; participate in design reviews.
  90 days: eligible for the on-call rotation (see On-Call Runbook, current version).

QUESTIONS

Ask in #eng-onboarding. Your buddy and manager are your first escalation path;
People Ops (Ingrid Solberg) owns everything compensation- and benefits-related.
