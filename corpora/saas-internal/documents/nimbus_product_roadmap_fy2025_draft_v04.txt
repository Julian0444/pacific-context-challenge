PROPOSED SEQUENCE (v0.4)

              Rationale: competitive buzz; demo-able at the June conference.

  Q3 FY2025   Warehouse-native mode — query customer data in place (Snowflake,
              BigQuery) instead of ingesting through Skyhook. Unlocks data-team
              buyers; reduces storage COGS.

              Held to Q4 because infra work competes with warehouse-native.
