NIMBUS ANALYTICS — ENGINEERING COMPENSATION BANDS 2024
People Operations | March 20, 2024 | RESTRICTED — MANAGERS AND ABOVE

OWNER: Ingrid Solberg, VP People | Approved by: Marcus Bell, CFO

REVISION NOTES

Supersedes Compensation Bands 2023 (March 2023). Key changes: bands raised
roughly 8–9% against market data, bonus target moved from 8% to 10%, a scheduled
annual equity refresh program replaces ad hoc refreshes, and three geographic
tiers are introduced.

BASE SALARY BANDS — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING (USD, ANNUAL, TIER 1)

  L3  Software Engineer          $128,000 – $152,000   (mid $140,000)
  L4  Senior Software Engineer   $158,000 – $186,000   (mid $172,000)
  L5  Staff Software Engineer    $192,000 – $224,000   (mid $208,000)
  EM1 Engineering Manager        $178,000 – $210,000   (mid $194,000)

SRE and Data keep the +5% premium at L4+ carried over from 2023.

GEOGRAPHIC TIERS (NEW IN 2024)

  Tier 1 (100%)  San Francisco Bay Area, New York metro, Seattle.
  Tier 2 (92%)   Other major US metros (Austin, Denver, Boston, LA, Chicago).
  Tier 3 (85%)   All other US locations.

Existing employees are grandfathered at their current tier; relocations re-tier
at the next review cycle.

BONUS AND EQUITY (2024 POLICY)

  — Company bonus target: 10% of base (was 8%).
  — ANNUAL equity refresh program: everyone rated Meets or above receives a
    refresh grant sized by level and rating band; vesting 4 years, quarterly.

PLACEMENT GUIDANCE

Unchanged from 2023: hire into the 25th–50th percentile; >75th percentile needs
VP People sign-off; out-of-band offers need CFO approval.
