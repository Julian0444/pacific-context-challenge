NIMBUS ANALYTICS — ON-CALL RUNBOOK
Version 2.0 | April 18, 2024 | INTERNAL — ENGINEERING

OWNER: Lena Fischer, Head of SRE

REVISION NOTES

Version 2.0 supersedes Version 1.0 (November 2023). Rewritten after the March 4,
2024 helios-db outage (see postmortem PM-2024-03). Key changes: Opsgenie replaces
PagerBot, a formal severity matrix and incident-commander role are introduced, and
helios-db failover is now automated.

ROTATION

One primary and one secondary engineer per week, per service group (Skyhook,
Prism, Gatekeeper, helios-db). Handoff moved to WEDNESDAYS at 10:00 in
#incidents, with a written handoff note. Paging runs through Opsgenie rotations;
PagerBot is decommissioned.

SEVERITY MATRIX

  SEV1  Customer-facing outage or data loss. Acknowledge within 5 minutes.
        Incident commander assigned immediately; status page update within
        15 minutes; exec notification is automatic via Opsgenie policy.
  SEV2  Degraded service, workaround exists. Acknowledge within 15 minutes.
  SEV3  Non-urgent defect with operational impact. Next business day.
  SEV4  Cosmetic or internal-only. Backlog.

ESCALATION (v2)

  1. Opsgenie pages the primary; unacknowledged SEV1/SEV2 pages escalate to the
     secondary after 5 minutes, then to the SRE manager on the escalation policy.
  2. Every SEV1 gets an incident commander (IC) who owns comms and coordination.
     The IC is not the person debugging.

DATABASE FAILOVER (helios-db)

Failover is AUTOMATED via the orchestrator deployed April 2024: replica promotion,
pgbouncer pool cycling, and DNS switch run as one supervised workflow. Measured
recovery time objective (RTO): 8 minutes. Manual override remains available to
SRE via Gatekeeper for split-brain scenarios.

POSTMORTEMS

Every SEV1/SEV2 requires a blameless postmortem within 5 business days using the
standard template. Action items get owners and due dates tracked in #incidents.
